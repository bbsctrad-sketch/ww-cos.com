Our Story

Two universes.
One obsession: purity.

WW Cosmétiques was born from a simple idea: beauty should be engineered with the same rigor as a science.

Most beauty brands sell promises. We chose to sell formulas — small-batch, lab-tested, ruthlessly pure. 99.1% isn't a marketing number. It's the floor we refuse to go below.

We split our world in two. HIM, for the modern man who wants tools that perform. HER, for the woman who draws her own line. Same lab. Same standard. Different rituals.

We launch with two products — one per universe — because we'd rather perfect one thing than sell ten. When you find a formula that works, the rest is just noise.

"The science of beauty."
